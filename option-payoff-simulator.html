Long Straddle (At-the-Money)
Profits from significant volatility in either direction. Consists of buying a Call and Put at the identical strike price (Kc = Kp). Higher IV increases premium cost, requiring larger moves to breakeven.
Payoff = max(S − Kc, 0) + max(Kp − S, 0) − (Pc + Pp)
